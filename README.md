# 🛡️ PayShield AI

### Explainable Payment Risk Intelligence

> **Predict. Explain. Decide.**

PayShield AI is an explainable payment-risk intelligence platform designed to help merchants identify potentially fraudulent transactions before they become financial losses.

Instead of treating fraud detection as a simple **YES/NO** decision, PayShield AI combines transaction and behavioral signals to generate a risk score, explain the reasons behind that score, recommend an action, and maintain an audit trail.

## 🎯 Problem

Digital payments are growing rapidly, but merchants face two major risks:

### Fraud Loss
Fraudulent transactions can result in:

- Direct financial losses
- Chargebacks and disputes
- Operational investigation costs
- Customer trust issues

### Customer Friction

Over-aggressive fraud prevention can incorrectly block legitimate customers, resulting in:

- False positives
- Failed payments
- Lost conversions
- Poor customer experience

Therefore, merchants need more than a simple fraud prediction.

They need to know:

> **How risky is this transaction? Why is it risky? What should we do about it?**
